# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS/JS.

## Users

- Primary: people and small businesses who need custom digital solutions. Their situation: they have a business need (a website, an online store, or a system to manage operations) and want a tailored build rather than a template.
- Secondary (when confirmed later): businesses considering a move from a simple web presence to an operational system.

## Product Purpose

Personal brand site for a solo software developer offering custom digital services: landing pages, simple websites, self-manageable websites, blogs, ecommerce, and bespoke systems (inventory, WMS, ERP, CRM, clinics, veterinary clinics) and mobile applications. Success means a visitor understands the full breadth of services and contacts the developer to start a project.

## Positioning

A single developer who accompanies the client end to end — from the first website through to the complete system that runs the business. The "one trusted partner across the whole journey" claim distinguishes the offer from agencies or template-only vendors.

## Operating Context

- Visitors land to evaluate capability and fit, then initiate contact for a custom build.
- Services span a clear spectrum of complexity, from low (landing page / simple site) to high (ERP, WMS, custom mobile apps). The page must communicate that range without diluting the "measured, tailored" message.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services offered: landing pages, simple websites, self-manageable (CMS-style) websites, blogs, ecommerce, custom systems (inventory, WMS, ERP, CRM), clinic/veterinary management systems, mobile applications.
- Delivery model: single developer, direct accompaniment.
- Language: content presented in Spanish is likely (audience + request in Spanish), but not yet confirmed for the final artifact.

## Brand Commitments

- Service categories and the end-to-end single-developer journey described above are binding.
- Undecided (do not invent): name/brand identity, logo, tone of voice, specific tools/stack claims, pricing, testimonials, case studies.

## Evidence on Hand

- No real imagery, portfolio pieces, testimonials, case studies, or client references exist in the project yet. Future work must not fabricate these; the developer must supply them.

## Product Principles

- Communicate the full service spectrum, from a single landing page to a full business system.
- Emphasize the human, end-to-end partnership as the core differentiator.
- Invite contact as the primary conversion action.
- Avoid inventing credentials, references, or technical claims not yet true.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- No product-specific accessibility requirement was established beyond standard web practice.
